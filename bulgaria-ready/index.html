Euro since 2026

Bulgaria joined the euro on 1 January 2026 at a fixed rate of 1.95583 leva. The lev stopped being legal tender on 1 February 2026 — every price you'll see quoted now is in euro.

Full Schengen member

Since 1 January 2025 there are no border checks by land, air or sea between Bulgaria and the rest of the Schengen area. Driving down with a car full of belongings is genuinely straightforward.

Irish vs British buyers

Your passport changes what you can buy

Since Brexit, British buyers are treated as non-EU nationals in Bulgaria. It doesn't stop you buying — but it changes the structure, the cost and the timeline, and estate agents don't always volunteer that.

What you're buying Irish / EU passport UK passport
An apartment In your own name In your own name
A house or building In your own name In your own name — the building only
The land it stands on In your own name Needs a Bulgarian company — an EOOD, about €1 of share capital, a few working days to register
Agricultural or forest land Restricted Restricted
Staying beyond 90 days Register locally, longer-term residence card Type D visa first, applied for before you travel, then a residence permit renewed annually
Permanent residence After 5 years After 5 years of continuous legal residence

This is general information, not legal advice. Bulgarian property and immigration rules change, and your own circumstances matter. BulgariaReady helps you understand the process and ask the right questions — always confirm the detail with a qualified Bulgarian lawyer before you sign or transfer money.

Questions

Moving to Bulgaria, briefly answered

Can a British citizen buy property in Bulgaria after Brexit?

Yes — apartments and buildings, in your own name, with no special permit. What you cannot do as a non-EU national is own the land in your own name. In practice that means an apartment is straightforward, while a village house with a garden usually involves registering a Bulgarian company to hold the plot.

What does it cost to live in Bulgaria?

Substantially less than the UK or Ireland, though the gap has narrowed and varies enormously between Sofia, the coast and inland villages. Since January 2026 everything is priced in euro, which makes comparison a good deal easier than it used to be.

Do I need a visa to move to Bulgaria from the UK?

For stays over 90 days in any 180, yes — a Type D long-stay visa, applied for at the Bulgarian embassy before you travel, followed by a residence permit once you're there. Irish and other EU citizens don't need one and register locally instead.
