Before you publish this

Everything highlighted below needs your real details. This is a solid working draft covering what GDPR requires for a site that collects email addresses — it is not legal advice, and if the app goes on to handle payments or richer personal data you should have a solicitor review it.

Who we are

BulgariaReady ("we", "us") provides free guides about moving to Bulgaria and a relocation planning app. The data controller is [YOUR LEGAL NAME OR COMPANY NAME], of [YOUR ADDRESS], [COUNTRY].

For any privacy question, or to exercise any of the rights below, contact us at [YOUR ZOHO EMAIL ADDRESS].

What we collect

If you sign up for our guides by email, we collect:

  • Your email address — required, so we can send you the guides
  • Your first name — optional, so we can address you properly
  • Which passport you hold, why you're interested in Bulgaria, and your budget range — all optional, so we can send you relevant material rather than everything

We do not collect payment details through this site. We do not use analytics, advertising pixels or tracking cookies on these guide pages.

If you also use the BulgariaReady app, it holds the information you enter into it — your profile, checklists, property viewing notes and any pet details. That is covered by the same policy and the same rights.

Why we're allowed to use it

Our lawful basis is your consent, given by ticking the box on the signup form. We do not add anyone to our list who has not asked to be added, and we use a confirmation email to check the address genuinely belongs to you.

You can withdraw consent at any time. Every email we send carries a one-click unsubscribe link, and withdrawing is as easy as giving it was.

What we use it for

  • Sending you the guides and updates you signed up for
  • Telling you about material changes to Bulgarian rules that affect movers
  • Deciding which guides to write next, based on aggregate patterns

We do not sell your data. We do not share it with estate agents, property developers, lawyers or any other third party who might want to sell you something. This matters to us: we take no commission on property, so we have no reason to pass you on.

Who processes it for us

Our email list is held and sent through Zoho Campaigns, operated by Zoho Corporation, which acts as our data processor under contract. Data is held on Zoho's [EU / your chosen region] servers.

Our website is served by [YOUR HOSTING PROVIDER], which may keep standard server logs including IP addresses for security purposes.

How long we keep it

For as long as you stay subscribed. If you unsubscribe, we keep a minimal record of the unsubscribe itself so we don't accidentally email you again — that is a legitimate interest and an industry-standard suppression list. Otherwise your details are deleted.

If you have not opened any of our emails for [24 months], we will remove you from the list.

Your rights

Under GDPR you have the right to:

  • Access — get a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Rectification — have anything inaccurate corrected
  • Erasure — have your data deleted
  • Restriction — ask us to stop processing it while a dispute is resolved
  • Portability — receive your data in a machine-readable format
  • Object — object to processing based on legitimate interests
  • Withdraw consent — at any time, without affecting what we did lawfully beforehand

Email [YOUR ZOHO EMAIL ADDRESS] and we'll respond within one month, as GDPR requires. There's no charge.

Complaints

If you think we've handled your data badly, please tell us first — we'd rather fix it. You also have the right to complain to a supervisory authority.

[If you are established in Ireland:] the Irish Data Protection Commission, at dataprotection.ie. If you are in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office, at ico.org.uk. Residents of other EU states may complain to their own national authority.

Cookies

These guide pages set no cookies and run no third-party scripts. We use your browser's local storage for one thing only: remembering that you've already signed up, so we don't show you the form again. That never leaves your device and we cannot read it.
